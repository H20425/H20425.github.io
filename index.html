Electroplating thickness monitor

Jan 2025 – Aug 2025

Plating thickness is usually checked after a run, when it's too late to fix. I researched ways to track it during the run, then designed a board that measures plating current over time and uses Faraday's law to estimate deposit thickness in real time.

I built three versions: Arduino-based, ESP-based and fully analog. After testing with the people who run the line and fixing what they found, I built four units of the improved board. All four are in daily production use.

RF sweep generator / scalar network analyzer

2026 · rev A

A sweep generator and scalar network analyzer covering 35 MHz to 4.4 GHz, for testing filters, amplifiers and cables. An ADF4351 PLL synthesizer makes the signal, a GVA-84+ amplifies it, and an HMC624A step attenuator sets the level from -16 to +4.7 dBm. On the input, an AD8318 log detector and a 16-bit ADC measure what comes back. It is a 4-layer board with 50 Ω coplanar waveguide traces. I simulated the PLL loop, phase noise and RF power budget, and wrote the RP2040 firmware and a Python app for the PC.

100 W buck-boost bench supply

2026 · rev A

A 0–30 V, 0–5 A bench supply that runs from a 5–36 V input or USB-C Power Delivery. An LT8390 4-switch buck-boost converter at 300 kHz drives an LC filter, with CV/CC control and hardware over-voltage protection. Two INA228 power monitors measure voltage and current, and an RP2040 runs a 2-inch LCD and potentiometers. I verified the design in LTspice before laying out the 4-layer board.

Opal: muscle-sensing wristband for skiers

Capstone (ENSC 405W/440) · team of 5 · 2026

Skiers can't reach their phone through gloves and layers. Opal reads forearm muscle signals (sEMG) and sends Bluetooth media commands like skip, play/pause and volume up and down.

  • I designed the power board (Li-ion charging, fuel gauge, buck-boost regulation) and the 2-layer flex electrode bands in KiCad, and co-designed the 4-layer ESP32-S3 + ADS1298 motherboard with the team.
  • Before the compact design we designed and built a test bench of ADS1298 boards (first version in picture) to prove the design works.
  • On one charge of a 1000 mAh cell the system ran 9 h 52 min in a freezer at about -20 °C, against an 8 h target.
